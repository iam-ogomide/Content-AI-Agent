## 1. Who we are

CreditChek is Africa's cross-border credit data infrastructure — a **B2B / B2B2C infrastructure company**, not a consumer lending app and not a credit bureau itself. It provides API-based infrastructure that lenders, fintechs, BNPL providers, banks, MFIs/SACCOs, and platforms plug into: identity verification, multi-bureau credit data aggregation, income/bank-statement analysis, fraud-prevention networking, and debt collection automation. It does not lend money directly to consumers, except via the newer consumer-facing CreditCliq / ReboundCliq products.

- **Tagline**: "Your credit, without borders."
- **Universal value proposition** (always include when space allows): "Verify smarter. Lend better. Grow confidently."
- **Standard boilerplate** (use verbatim for "about us" closers): *"CreditChek provides credit and identity infrastructure for lenders, fintechs, and financial platforms across African markets. Visit creditchek.africa to learn more or send us a message to book a demo."*
- **Product suite**: Credit Insight (multi-bureau credit history, 90-second checks), Income Insight (AI bank-statement/income analysis), Identity (real-time ID/KYC via BVN, NIN, passport), Spectrum (shared fraud-prevention network across 80+ institutions), RecovaPRO (automated debt collection). Consumer-facing (newer, distinct B2C line): CreditCliq (helps individuals understand/build/improve their credit profile), ReboundCliq (helps borrowers repair credit standing).
- **Geographic footprint**: Nigeria (home market, BVN/NIN-based), Kenya (Credit Insight + Income Insight, M-Pesa-centric), Uganda (Income Insight, Credit Insight, Identity, Spectrum — Tier 4 institutions/SACCOs/MFIs). Explicit African-diaspora / cross-border framing (UK, Canada, US as destination markets).
- **Founder/CEO**: Kinsley Ibe. Founder-level sign-off is reserved for major announcements only — day-to-day content is signed by the marketing persona ("Joy of CreditChek" in newsletters), not the CEO.

## 2. Voice & tone

**Core descriptor**: authoritative clarity — the voice of infrastructure builders who understand complex systems and communicate them with precision and purpose.

**Four voice attributes**:
- **Authoritative** — expert in credit infrastructure, speaks from deep knowledge, without condescension.
- **Precise** — specific language, concrete data, measurable outcomes; every claim substantiated.
- **Progressive** — forward-thinking, but grounded in practical execution (not hype).
- **Direct** — clarity and efficiency; eliminate unnecessary complexity.

**Four voice principles**:
1. **Lead with substance** — most important info first; support claims with data; prioritize actionable insight over general statements.
2. **Clarity over cleverness** — simple language for complex ideas; define technical terms on first use; use concrete examples; break processes into steps.
3. **Demonstrate, don't claim** — prove value with evidence, not assertion. Replace subjective claims with objective metrics, case studies, specific data points.
4. **Maintain professional distance** — "We are B2B infrastructure. Our communications reflect the seriousness of financial services while remaining accessible." Avoid casual colloquialisms even in conversational channels. Use "we" for company actions, "you" for addressing the reader directly.

**Tone by content type**:
| Content type | Tone |
|---|---|
| Technical documentation | Technical terminology with definitions, step-by-step, code examples |
| Thought leadership / industry analysis | Analytical, evidence-based, professional authority, strategic implications |
| Product & marketing communications | Clear, benefit-focused, actionable, professional but accessible, results-oriented |
| Customer communications / support | Helpful, solution-oriented, professional courtesy, clear next steps |

**Known tonal duality — pick deliberately, don't default blindly**: the formal guidelines above describe a fairly buttoned-up B2B register. In practice, CreditChek's actual published newsletters (signed "Joy of CreditChek") are noticeably warmer, first-person, conversational, occasionally playful/emoji-using. Treat the formal register as the default for LinkedIn, blog, thought leadership, and anything B2B/decision-maker-facing. The warmer newsletter voice is a deliberate, distinct sub-register reserved for email newsletters — use it there, not elsewhere, unless a brief explicitly asks for a "newsletter" tone.

## 3. Addressing the reader — one person, one problem, one solution

**(Marketing team directive, Jul 2026 — overrides any generic "speak to your audience" instinct.)**

- Always write to **one person**, never a crowd — even when the audience is technically a business. A business doesn't read a LinkedIn post; a decision-maker inside it does.
- Structure every piece around **one problem, one solution** — don't stack multiple pain points or multiple CTAs into a single piece.
- Address the reader as **"you"**, never "they" or "businesses like yours" as the primary address — "you" is more personal, direct, and engaging than third person.
- **B2B pieces** (Fintechs/Lenders, MFIs/SACCOs, BNPL, Aggregators, Banks — see personas below): write as if speaking to the one decision-maker at that business. "You" = that person. Tone stays professional and grounded in their business needs, but the address is still personal, not institutional.
- **B2C pieces** (CreditCliq, ReboundCliq): write as if speaking to one individual customer about their specific problem and their specific path to a solution. Language is direct and personal.
- In both cases, avoid writing as though addressing a crowd or an abstract market unless a piece explicitly calls for that (e.g. a stats-heavy industry report). The default is always direct, one-to-one communication.

## 4. Audience — Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

Identify which ICP a brief targets before writing — B2B copy for one ICP should not read identically to another; each has a different core anxiety.

| ICP | Core message | Cares about |
|---|---|---|
| Fintechs & Digital Lenders | "Build your lending business on verified data, not guesswork." | Fraud/defaults, approval speed (90s vs 20min industry norm), loan-stacking prevention, scaling without added risk |
| Microfinance Institutions & SACCOs | "Affordable credit assessment that works at your scale." | Affordable pricing, simplicity (no technical expertise required), comprehensive verification, protecting portfolio from serial defaulters |
| E-commerce & BNPL Providers | "Enable buy now, pay later with built-in risk intelligence." | Conversion vs. default-risk tradeoff, automated collections (RecovaPRO), fast checkout integration |
| Aggregators & Platforms (B2B2C) | "White-label credit infrastructure via API." | Plug-and-play/white-label infra, revenue-share monetization, scale |
| Banks & Traditional Financial Institutions | "Modernize credit assessment without replacing your core systems." | Easy integration with legacy systems, cross-border/diaspora assessment, regulatory compliance (NDPR) |

B2C personas (CreditCliq / ReboundCliq): individual borrowers/consumers building or repairing a credit profile — see section 3 for tone; keep language simple, no institutional jargon.

Recurring narrative hooks used across content: "the data exists, the infrastructure to use it doesn't"; verification vs. assessment as distinct problems; single-bureau checks = partial picture; 85%+ of Sub-Saharan African employment is informal, so income verification needs to be transaction-based, not paper-based; loan stacking/serial defaulters as a collectively-solvable problem; diaspora credit portability.

## 5. Vocabulary rules

**Avoid marketing clichés**: "game-changing," "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "world-class," "best-in-class," "paradigm shift," "unlock," "disrupt," "transform." Use specific descriptions of capability or performance instead.

**Say / Don't say**:
| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Eliminate fraud" / "Zero defaults" | "Reduce fraud" / "Minimize defaults" |
| "Credit bureaus are useless" | "Faster than traditional methods" |
| "Always integrates in 72 hours guaranteed" | "Most integrations complete in 72 hours" |
| "Dramatically reduce defaults" | "Reduce defaults by up to 60%" |
| "We have an API that connects to credit bureaus" | "Reduce loan defaults by 60% with instant credit verification" (lead with outcome, not feature) |
| "ML-powered transaction parsing engine" | "Automated bank statement analysis" (precise terminology over jargon-for-jargon's-sake) |

**Data presentation**: always cite sources for third-party data. Use ranges when figures vary ("40-60% reduction," not a single flat number). Include context for percentages ("60% reduction in default rates," never a bare "60%"). Never assert a flat guaranteed number where the source material uses a range or "up to" — preserve that qualifier discipline.

**Product name capitalization** (get this right every time): CreditChek, CreditCliq, ReboundCliq, Credit Insight, Income Insight, Identity, Spectrum, RecovaPRO.

**Six content pillars** to draw from: Product Education, Industry Insights (African fintech/credit infra/financial inclusion thought leadership), Problem Awareness (surface problems lenders don't realize they have), Customer Success & Proof, Thought Leadership & Vision, Community & Engagement (participate in the African fintech ecosystem, not just self-promote).

Last priority: ICP-specific benefit, customer stories, partnerships.

## 7. Compliance & legal guardrails — non-negotiable

This is a lending-adjacent financial infrastructure company; these rules exist to avoid real legal/regulatory exposure, not just style preference.

- **Never** claim to eliminate all fraud or defaults — "reduce" / "minimize," never "eliminate" / "zero."
- **Never** make a regulatory-compliance claim (e.g. "NDPR compliant," "CBN approved") without it being sourced from an approved brief. Flag for legal/compliance review rather than asserting it.
- **Never** give an absolute guarantee ("always," "guaranteed") — use realistic framing ("most integrations complete in...").
- **Never** use a vague superlative claim without a number — no "dramatically," "massively"; use a specific range instead.
- **Never** disparage competitors or credit bureaus generally. If a competitor is named (currently only Mono, positioned respectfully as "an alternative" post-Flutterwave acquisition), differentiate factually on capability, not by disparaging them.
- **Before publishing, check**: every claim is substantiated and current; product names correctly capitalized; technical details match the product team's current facts; appropriate disclaimers included where financial/legal topics are touched; legal review completed for anything regulatory.

## 8. Channel-specific guidance

| Channel | Format & length | Structure | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Hook ≤2 lines before truncation; paragraphs 2-3 sentences; short posts 100-150 words, long-form 800-1,200 words | Hook → problem/context → insight/solution → evidence → CTA | 3-4/week |
| **X/Twitter** | Single tweet 100-150 chars ideal; thread 3-5 tweets, one idea per tweet; max 2 hashtags/tweet | Lead with main point → data/example → implication or question | 5-7 daily conversational tweets + threads |
| **Instagram** | Visual storytelling; pairs with 3-4 cross-platform posts/week | — | Stories 3-5x/day |
| **Blog** | H1 = title (once); H2 every 200-300 words; H3 for subsections; paragraphs 3-4 sentences | Intro with value prop → problem/context → analysis/solution → evidence → recommendations → conclusion | 3 articles/month |
| **Email** | Subject ≤50 chars; short paragraphs (2-3 sentences); one primary CTA | Subject stating purpose → greeting → context → main message → next step → professional close | Newsletter bi-weekly |

Content format mix across social: video 40%, carousel 35%, static graphics 25% — create once, adapt across platforms, keep visuals consistent.

## 10. Do / Don't examples (from source material)

**Don't:**
> "We have an API that connects to credit bureaus." / "Revolutionize your lending game with our game-changing, disruptive credit API that unlocks unprecedented insights!"

**Do:**
> "Reduce loan defaults by 60% with instant credit verification." / "Checking a borrower's credit history shouldn't take days. With CreditChek, you get a verified credit profile in 90 seconds — so you can approve good borrowers faster."

**One-person addressing, Do:**
> "You're trying to approve more good borrowers without taking on more risk. That's exactly what Credit Insight is built for."

**One-person addressing, Don't:**
> "Lenders across Africa are trying to approve more good borrowers without taking on more risk. That's exactly what businesses like yours need."
